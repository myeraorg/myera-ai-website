LEARN YOUR HARDWARE

Computer Hardware Gallery

Identify common computer parts and understand their basic purpose.

CMOS Battery

A coin-cell battery that helps maintain the system clock and certain firmware settings when the PC is off.

Graphics Card (GPU)

Processes graphics and supports display output, gaming and GPU-accelerated tasks.

Cooling Fan

Moves air to help remove heat from a CPU, laptop or other computer components.

BIOS Chip

Stores firmware used during startup to initialize hardware and begin booting the computer.

Note: Part appearance and compatibility vary by device. Always verify the exact model before replacing a component.
